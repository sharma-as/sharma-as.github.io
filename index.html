Case Log: EVTOL Drone Mass Optimization

Mathematical Context: Iterative Finite Sizing, Shear Modulus Limit

Problem Scope & Criteria

Calculated Results

  • Extended continuous simulated hover times by 10%.

Case Log: Francis Turbine Component Fatigue

Investigation Range: FEM Analysis, Dynamic Pressure Fields
Software Framework: ANSYS Mechanical, SolidWorks FEA

Problem Scope & Criteria

Francis hydro-turbines encounter volatile dynamic pressure drops across operating rotor surfaces, inducing extreme multi-axial structural fatigue. This investigation aimed to isolate localized stress regions prone to micro-cracking propagation.

Simulation & Iterations

Applying fluid pressure results as localized surface boundary forces, we constructed multi-node solid meshes in ANSYS. Evaluated critical dynamic regions, tracking fillet modifications to smooth load transfer paths.

Calculated Results

  • Isolated extreme fatigue stress spots at blade root fillets, reducing fatigue risk.
  • Raised dynamic mesh convergence precision metrics by 30%.
